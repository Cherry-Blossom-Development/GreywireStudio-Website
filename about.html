About

Greywire Studio

Greywire Studio designs guitar effects pedals that work as a connected system. It's part of Cherry Blossom Development, a small software and hardware company.

Why we're building this

Lots of companies make great pedals. Very few make pedals that know about each other. We think the board should follow the song: set it up once, then play the show with one foot.

The names follow a wire theme. Greywire is the brand, Linework is the series of linked pedals, and each pedal is named after something from the world of power lines and signals.

Where things stand

FlashoverCircuit designed and simulated, circuit boards laid out. A breadboard prototype is being built to hear it before boards are made.
SwitchyardFeatures decided and the control protocol drafted. Breadboard prototype planned.
Switchyard with effectsPlanning a version with built-in digital effects.
App and networkBeing designed alongside the pedals.

Nothing is for sale yet. Names, features and looks may change before launch.
