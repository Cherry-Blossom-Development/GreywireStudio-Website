Linework series · in development

Guitar pedals that
work together.

Linework pedals sound great on their own. Link them to Switchyard and every pedal on your board changes with one tap, set up ahead of time for every part of every song.

How Linework works See the pedals

The idea

Plenty of companies make pedals.
We make a pedalboard that's connected.

01

Every pedal stands alone

Each Linework pedal is a complete pedal with its own knobs and footswitch. Plug in, play, no phone required.

02

One tap changes everything

Switchyard steps through your setlist: intro, verse, chorus, solo. Each step sets every linked pedal at once.

03

Your phone sets it up

Build setlists and sounds in the app at home. Switchyard stores them, so the show goes on even if the phone doesn't.
